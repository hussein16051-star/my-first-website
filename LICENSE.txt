<h2>Propagating Mint</h2>
<p>I enjoy gardening and rooting mint cuttings in water. This process is very simple and rewarding for anyone living in Glasgow. You only need a glass, some fresh water, and a healthy mint stem. First, remove the bottom leaves from your cutting. Then, place it in the water and wait for roots to appear. It usually takes about a week to see progress. I find this hobby very relaxing after a long day of work. It is a great way to grow your own fresh herbs at home without needing a large garden or expensive equipment.</p>

<img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1585915434531-155104d493a7?w=400" alt="Plant roots">

<h2>My Simple Steps</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Cut a healthy mint stem.</li>
  <li>Place stem in clean water.</li>
  <li>Change water every two days.</li>
  <li>Wait for roots to grow.</li>
</ul>
