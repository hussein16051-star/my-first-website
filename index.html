My Plant Care Guide

Rose (ورد جوري)

Roses are beautiful flowering shrubs that require plenty of sunlight and regular pruning to thrive. They need well-drained soil and consistent watering at the base of the plant.

  • Sunlight: At least 6 hours of direct sun.
  • Water: Deep watering twice a week.
  • Care Tip: Remove dead flowers to encourage new growth.

Bramble / Blackberry (العُلّيق)

Brambles are very common in Scotland. They are hardy plants that produce delicious blackberries in the late summer. They grow very fast and can spread across a garden quickly.

  • Sunlight: Partial shade to full sun.
  • Soil: Can grow in almost any soil type.
  • Care Tip: Prune them back in the winter to keep them under control.

Mint (نعناع)

Mint is a wonderful herb that is very easy to grow. It is best to keep it in a pot because its roots spread very fast and can take over your entire garden soil.

  • Sunlight: Prefers bright, indirect light.
  • Water: Keep the soil consistently moist.
  • Care Tip: If it starts to wilt, give it plenty of water and it should bounce back quickly.
